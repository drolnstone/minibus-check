WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. index.html (driver app):
   - The pickup pop-up. It comes up when the next booked stop's estimate is
     a minute or less away, the same test that turns the passenger page red
     with "Be at your stop", read off the estimate the board already
     carries. Once up for a stop it stays up until Picked up or Nobody here,
     through every poll. Only on the phone driving the run.
   - A bus ahead of its estimate: tap the next stop in the list and the
     pop-up comes up there and then.
   - A next stop with no estimate (no signal, or a run with no offset yet)
     cannot say when "Be at your stop" comes, so that stop's own row keeps
     Nobody there and Picked up, as a stop gone past already does.
   - Picked up and Nobody here are the same taps as before: same PIN gate,
     same refusal while the bus moves, same record.
   - Call passenger rings the number booked with the seat. Refused while
     the bus moves. A booking with no number (some coordinator bookings)
     has no button.
   - The strip at the foot of the list keeps its line ("2 people waiting at
     ...") and loses its two buttons.

2. server/worker.js w2.46.0: a new action, stoppeople. It hands the numbers
   booked on a route this Sunday to one phone only: the driver of the run
   out on that route now, with his PIN. A wrong PIN, another driver, the
   other route, no run or an ended run get nothing. The board every phone
   reads still carries no number.

3. sw.js, sunday/sw.js, coord/sw.js, sunday/index.html, coord/index.html:
   the version only.

4. Tests. tests/suites/55-stop-people.mjs (the server's half). Browser
   check T27 (the pop-up, start to finish); T4 rewritten for the foot of the
   list with no buttons in it.
