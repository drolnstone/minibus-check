WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. THE VEHICLE LOG. A new tab, and a Buses screen in the coordinator's app
   (each bus's dates, its log, Record something, Correct, and the jobs the
   last walkaround asked for with a Done button). All that is needed is what
   it was; the day done is today unless changed, and everything else may be
   left empty. Booked ahead puts a garage booking on the log and moves no
   date. A mistake is put right with a correction or a withdrawal, a new row
   naming the old one, which stays struck through. (coord/index.html
   paintBuses, paintBus, recordFor, correctEntry, jobDone; worker.js actVlog,
   actVfix, actJob, coordVehiclesView; Code.gs coordVlog, coordVfix,
   coordJob, vlogShelf.)

2. THE NEXT DUE DATE. Service, twelve months from the day it was done. MOT,
   tested within a month less a day before it ran out keeps its date a year
   on; otherwise a year from the test less a day. Insurance and permit,
   renewed in the two months up to expiry keeps the anniversary; otherwise
   twelve months from the renewal. A date from the certificate or policy
   always wins. A service can be lined up with an MOT six to thirteen months
   off. The same rules are in worker.js, Code.gs and coord/index.html
   (rnNextDue), and a test holds the three together over every day for three
   years. The new date is on every phone the moment it is recorded.

3. HISTORY. A new tab, only ever added to: every change to a bus's due
   dates (from the app, a row typed on the Vehicle Log, the Buses tab typed
   on, pasted into or changed by a script, caught at the next sync), every
   status a defect is given and what was done about it, a defect reopened
   with the day it had first been closed, the log's own corrections, and
   jobs marked done. An edit on History is itself written down. The
   coordinator's Defects screen shows each defect's trail.

4. THE BUSES TAB'S DATES take a date and nothing else, from the first sync.
   A cell already holding text that is not a date is written on History as
   unreadable rather than read as blank.

5. WHERE THE LOG STARTS. One Estimated row for each date on the Buses tab, a
   year back from it, the last service taken as done with the last MOT.
   Correct any whose real date you know.

6. IS EVERYTHING WORKING? holds what the phones have against the tabs, as
   it now does for the Message button: the due dates every driver app warns
   from against the Buses tab, and the Vehicle Log the coordinator's Buses
   screen shows against the tab. A difference is a sync that has not landed
   (Needs attention: Send everything); a date cell that cannot be read is
   listed To do. (Code.gs vehicleHealth; worker.js alertRoll's busDates and
   vlogHeld, dates and counts only.)

7. THE DRIVER APP'S TOP BAR. Before a bus is chosen it said VEHICLE CHECK,
   CHOOSE YOUR NAME TO BEGIN over the way into the whole app, with "Fleet" in
   an empty number plate that read as a button that did not work and three
   unlit lights that read as a page still loading. Now, until a bus is
   chosen, it names the app (DOMINION TRANSPORT, as the home screen icon
   does) or the screen (DRIVING ROTA, STOPS AND BOOKINGS), over the church
   before anybody signs in and the driver's name after, as the coordinator's
   app does. The plate and the lights come with the bus, and the title beside
   the plate says VEHICLE CHECK instead of the registration a second time.
