Server only. Coordinators hear about booking changes:
1. A coordinator's Book, Edit or Cancel goes to the other coordinators as
   "Change by (name)", e.g. "Sun 15 Nov. Changed 3 seats at Fountains Road
   by Stanley Close to 1 seat, number ending 748." (email if no phone).
2. Every booking alert to passengers goes on the Alerts list, e.g.
   "Booking changed by Bro Asim: Sun 15 Nov: ... To 1 passenger phone."
3. A coordinator's Edit or Cancel reaches the passenger as "Booking changed
   by (name)" / "Seat cancelled by (name)". A passenger's own cancel, to
   their other phones, stays "Seat cancelled".

Merging is the whole deploy. Nothing to do on the phone.
