The estimate from where the bus is. While a run is open and the Stops
screen is on, the driving phone sends its location with every board
poll. Once a minute the server asks the sheet, which asks Google Maps,
how long the drive is to the next booked stop in traffic; every ten
minutes each leg after it. Every stop's time, the driver's pop-up and
the passengers' page and alerts all use it. No location for two
minutes, or no answer from Google: the estimate from the stop taps, as
before.

Pickup pop-up: on every tap the held estimates go until the server has
the tap. The estimate only raises a pop-up within 1 km of the stop's
pin, and one it raised goes back down if the stop moves more than a
minute away.

The sheet's health report says whether Google's traffic times are coming
through.
