MINIBUS
=======
  pages v1.89.1 · server w2.30.1 · sheet v1.92.0 (unchanged)

A fix, to go live before Sunday 4 October. The driver app calls a stop tap
"pickup" (Picked up) or "empty" (Nobody there). The live server looked for
"picked" and "none" instead in three places, so a real tap never woke the
passengers further down the route, never showed on the coordinator's Run
record, and Add on the Run record could put a second time beside the
driver's own. The sheet always read the taps correctly; nothing on it needs
putting right.
