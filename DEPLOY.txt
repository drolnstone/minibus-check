WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. FROM THE COORDINATOR'S APP TO THE DRIVER APP, SIGNED IN.
   The driver app's Coordinator button already carried a checked PIN to
   the coordinator's app. Now the coordinator's app does the same the other
   way, once signed in: the Driver app link lands on the driver app's hub,
   signed in, and a new Vehicle check at the foot of the menu goes on to
   choosing the bus. The name and PIN go once, in that tab only, and are
   refused after a minute. The driver app checks the PIN as it checks one
   keyed there; a wrong one stays on the name screen.

2. THE COORDINATOR TITLES ARE A SETTING.
   Apps Script, Project Settings, Script Properties, COORDINATOR_ROLES: the
   titles as they appear in the Role column, separated by commas, the one
   people should ring first. For example

       Coordinator, Minister in Charge

   Blank or missing uses exactly that. After changing it, Send everything
   to the live server now. The live server passes the titles to every page,
   so config.js is not edited and nothing is redeployed. They decide who may
   authorise a stopped bus, who is offered the full inspection, who may open
   the coordinator's app, and who people are told to ring. Capitals and
   doubled spaces in the Role column no longer matter.

3. WHO PEOPLE RING FOLLOWS THOSE TITLES.
   It looked for the word Coordinator alone, so a church with other titles
   showed nobody. It now takes whoever COORDINATOR_EMAIL names among
   everybody holding one of the titles, and otherwise the first person
   holding the first title, then the second. Here that changes nothing: the
   Coordinator comes before the Minister in Charge.
