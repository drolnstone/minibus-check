WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. FROM THE COORDINATOR'S APP TO THE DRIVER APP, SIGNED IN.
   The driver app's Coordinator button already carried a checked PIN to
   the coordinator's app. Now the coordinator's app does the same the other
   way, once signed in: the Driver app link lands on the driver app's hub,
   signed in, and a new Vehicle check at the foot of the menu goes on to
   choosing the bus. The name and PIN go once, in that tab only, and are
   refused after a minute. The driver app checks the PIN as it checks one
   keyed there; a wrong one stays on the name screen.

2. WHO PEOPLE RING FOLLOWS AUTHORISER_ROLES.
   It looked for the word Coordinator alone, so a church with other titles
   showed nobody. It now takes whoever COORDINATOR_EMAIL names among every
   role in AUTHORISER_ROLES, and otherwise the first person holding the
   first role in the list, then the second. Here that changes nothing: the
   Coordinator comes before the Minister in Charge.
