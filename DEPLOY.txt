One phone per run (Asim, 12 Oct).

A live run belongs to the phone that started it. While that phone is
talking to the server, another phone signed in as the same driver:
  - is refused at sign-in: "Your run is open on another phone."
  - cannot mark stops, Undo or end the run; its taps are refused.
Once the run's phone has said nothing for 5 minutes, the next phone of his
to sign in or open Stops and bookings takes the run over, from the last
stop marked. The old phone, when it comes back, lets the run go:
"Run moved to another phone." Real runs only, not rehearsals.

Pages and Worker. Code.gs unchanged.
