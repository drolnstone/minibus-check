Bus used: a weekday trip, recorded with no check.

Driver App: a Bus used button under Vehicle check. The bus, Today or
Yesterday, mileage when taken (if noted), mileage when left, fuel
showing. Saved under the signed-in name; a ✓ card on the first screen
that day.

Coordinator App: Record something on the first screen, under Bookings.
Choose the bus, then the Record form, with the buses at its top. A Bus
used chip: day, who drove, mileage taken (if noted), mileage left, fuel.

Both go on the Vehicle Log as What "Bus used" (two new columns, Driven
by and Mileage when taken). Nobody is told. The bus's next walkaround is
measured from the mileage left, and the coordinators are told only if
it differs. A mileage when taken stands in for a Sunday parked reading
that never came (Parked at: From Bus used). The fuel bars show the Bus
used fuel when it is the newest.
