Hand over asks, and a breakdown bus.

1. No signal. A driver's Hand over ask made with no signal is kept on his
   phone ("Hand over to ... Not sent yet: no signal") and sent by itself
   when signal comes back.
2. No answer. An ask stays until a coordinator answers it. While nobody
   has, every coordinator is reminded at 5, 10 and 15 minutes ("Reminder:
   North: hand over asked"), on their phones and in Alerts.
3. Breakdown. Coordinator app, Sunday screen, Run, Hand over: pick the
   driver (the one holding it keeps it: Change the bus), then the bus:
   Same bus, one of our buses (nothing more), or Hired (reg and seats).
   No walkaround. The run's reg and seats follow it; the run record and
   the Rota note say "Bus AB12 CDE (hired, 14 seats) (was YS70 PWE)".
   Approving a driver's ask mid-run has the same bus step.
   Parked mileage stays with the bus the run started in.
