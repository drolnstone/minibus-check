Plan the rota (Coordinator App, Rota, "Plan the rota"):
- From: the first Sunday the plan decides.
- North and South: a list of names each, repeated Sunday after Sunday.
  Up, down, take off, Add a name. A name may be in a list more than once,
  and on both lists. Not active names are marked and refused on Save.
- Preview: each Sunday's two names; new ones say "was ...". Tap one name,
  then another, and they swap ("changed"). Undo swaps puts them back.
- Kept as they are: a Sunday with a cover, a request, a route called off,
  or PROTECTED. Shown faded.
- The same driver on both routes on one Sunday is refused in bold red.
- Save writes every Sunday that changes in one go. Coordinators get one
  "Change by" line; drivers whose Sunday changes in the next 7 days get it
  too, and the sheet's usual duty email.
- The sheet keeps the plan. Sundays the rota adds later, and "Rebuild
  future Sundays from the pattern", follow it from its first Sunday. Rebuild
  leaves any Sunday changed by hand after the plan was saved. Primary
  order on the Drivers tab is not used from that Sunday on.
- Active / Not active stays on the Drivers screen (Edit).

Merging is the whole deploy.
