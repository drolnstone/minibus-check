WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. COORDINATOR ALERTS ON EVERY COORDINATOR'S PHONE.
   Beside each email the coordinator is sent, the same alert goes to the
   phones: a bus stopped, a defect, an advisory or something to arrange on a
   check, a bus authorised to run, a rota request, a bus that went out
   unchecked, a route overbooked. It goes even with no COORDINATOR_EMAIL set.

   Short on purpose: what happened, and to which bus or Sunday. A phone joins
   the list by typing a name, with no PIN, so the detail stays in the email
   and in the coordinator's app, which the alert opens.

   A stopped bus goes at any hour. Everything else waits out the quiet
   hours, 21:00 to 08:00, and goes at 08:00. The person an alert is about is
   not woken by it. Each alert goes once, however many times it is handed
   over, and a stopped bus that has been authorised by the time the phone
   reads it says so.

2. THE TEST MENU ITEM TESTS BOTH.
   "Test email, to you only" is now "Test email and coordinators' phones",
   and sends a phone alert beside the email.
