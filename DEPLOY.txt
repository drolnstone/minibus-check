MOVE A STOP ONTO A ROUTE WITH 2 TRIPS
Move a stop (or Put back) onto a route split into Trip 1 and Trip 2 asks:
  Trip 1   as now: after a Trip 1 stop, at a time you type.
  Trip 2   after a Trip 2 stop, or first on Trip 2. No time to type: it
           is worked out with the rest of Trip 2's times.
Any time of the morning, until that trip is over, and not behind a stop
the bus has already been past.
Passengers at that stop:
  Trip 1        told at once, as now, with the time typed.
  Trip 2        "Sun 22 Nov: the North bus picks you up on its second
                trip, about 11:25. After service, the South bus home."
                Before 09:45 they hear it at 09:45 with Trip 2's other
                passengers; after 09:45, at once. Once only.
The 2 trips card shows the moved stop in its trip, without To trip 1/2.
To change its trip: Put back, then Move a stop again. Stops moved shows
"North, Trip 2".

Merging is the whole deploy.
