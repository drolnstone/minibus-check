Stop waiting time learned from real runs. The live estimate used to add
75 seconds at every stop the bus calls at. From now on the server measures
how long the bus stands at each stop, from the driving phone's location
(first fix within 80 m of the stop's pin, first fix after that beyond it),
at stops marked Picked up. Once a stop has 3 such stands, the estimate uses
the middle of its last 8; until then, 75 seconds as before. Nobody here
waits are not counted. Nothing on any screen changes, no setting is added,
and the Bus Stops timetable is not touched. The first Sundays after the
merge only collect; estimates change from about the third Sunday.

Merging is the whole deploy.
