Audit, 4 Nov: the high points (1 to 3).

1. Alerts under a name. A phone signs up for a driver's reminders only
   with his PIN key (driver app), and for coordinator alerts only with the
   coordinator's PIN (coordinator app). Phones already signed up stay as
   they are. A passenger's phone carries no driver's name.
2. A live run. The board poll takes a run over (after 5 quiet minutes)
   only from a phone with the driver's PIN key; the phone that holds the
   run carries on as before.
3. Coordinators' PIN. A coordinator still on the default PIN (last four
   of the phone number) keys it once more, sees "Set your own PIN.", and
   the Change PIN sheet opens with it filled in; the new PIN signs him
   straight in. The same default is refused for Authorise, End it and an
   email's action link ("That PIN is still the default."). Drivers keep
   their default.

Merging is the whole deploy.
