WHAT GOES TO THE COORDINATORS
-----------------------------

Title, then words, for each batch:

  Sent: Booking reminders        Book your seat: 12 passenger phones.
                                 (Saturday adds) You are booked: 5
                                 passenger phones.
  Sent: Your bus today           North: 5 passenger phones. South: 3
                                 passenger phones.
  Sent: North: You are driving   To Bro Moses: 1 phone.
  today                          (and the same for each other driver
                                 reminder: time to set off, not gone out,
                                 end the trip, stopped, authorised, off)
  Sent: North: No bus today      To 6 passenger phones.
  Sent: North: No word yet that  To 6 passenger phones.
  the bus has left church        (5 minutes past departure, no Start)
  Sent: North: The bus has gone  To 1 passenger phone.
  past Grace Rd                  (a booked stop passed unmarked, once a
                                 stop, booked with the app or without)
  Sent: Duty emails              Bro Moses, North Liverpool. Bro Adesina,
                                 South Liverpool.
                                 (or "None sent." and why)
  Sent: <the email's subject>    Email to Bro Moses.
                                 (any other email to a driver)

- Once each: a batch swept again a minute later is not reported twice.
- A batch that reached nobody still comes, with 0.
- Of the live updates during a run, only the two critical ones above.
  The bus has left church and on its way go to passengers only.
- Whoever the send went to is left out (a coordinator driving that day).
- Emails only to coordinator addresses are not repeated.
- Not held for the quiet hours, as the send itself was not.
- The Alerts list still shows each one under What went out; these add
  nothing to the bell's count.

THE PICKUP POP-UP
-----------------

Up a minute out by the estimate, as before, or when the phone has been
stopped for three seconds within 150 metres of the next booked stop's pin,
whichever comes first. Not while moving, on a fix vaguer than 150 metres,
on a phone that does not report its speed, or at a stop with no pin.
Tapping the stop's row still opens it too. Browser check T30.
