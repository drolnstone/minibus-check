SECOND TRIP FOR OVERFLOW
At 09:31 on a Sunday, a route booked over its bus's seats (after any bus
swap) is split into Trip 1 and Trip 2 on the same bus.
  Trip 1   whole stops from the farthest out, until the next would not
           fit; then Church, as now.
  Trip 2   the rest, farthest to closest, once Trip 1 has ended at Church.
09:31    coordinators and the route's driver get one alert: each trip's
         stops and seats, and Trip 2's times.
09:31 to 09:45, before Trip 1 starts: on the Coordinator App's Sunday
         screen a coordinator can move a stop to the other trip, or press
         One trip. Drivers cannot. Nothing done, it stands.
09:45    Trip 2's passengers: "Second trip. You are on the second trip.
         Your bus is due about 11:25." Their screen keeps the time up to
         date. Nobody hears about trips at 07:30.
Church   Trip 1 ends as now. The Driver App offers Start trip 2. Not
         started 10 minutes after Trip 1 ended: one "Time for Trip 2" to
         the driver.
Trip 2   works as Trip 1: left Church, Be at your stop, Nobody there,
         No word yet (from its estimated departure). No coordinator late
         start alert for Trip 2. No third trip.
Trip 2's times: Trip 1 at Church plus 5 minutes, then Google's drive
times stop to stop plus each stop's learned wait; without Google, the
straight-line drive from Church and the timetable's gaps.
The run report lists Trip 1 and Trip 2 in one email.

HAND OVER: MORNING ONLY OR FULL DAY
The last step of a hand over (and of approving a driver's request) asks:
  Morning only   back to the driver it came from once the morning's last
                 trip has ended (Trip 2 on a split day): the Rota cover
                 goes back, and the parked mileage, fuel and return are
                 theirs again.
  Full day       as before: the new driver's to the end.

ALSO
A stop moved to the other route for one Sunday no longer gives the next
Sunday's bookings at that stop a "New time" alert.

Merging is the whole deploy.
