MINIBUS
=======
  pages v1.86.0 · server w2.25.0 · sheet v1.88.0

The code no longer carries this church's buses, stops, kerbs, renewal
dates or bus pairing. The spreadsheet is the only place they live. Plus a
"Setting up for a new church" checklist in README.md. And two fixes to the
coordinator's app: it asks to be turned upright, and its top bar no longer
slides under the clock after a trip to the driver app.

WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. NOTHING OF THIS CHURCH'S IN THE CODE.
   SEED_BUSES, SEED_STOPS, STOP_PINS and BUS_DATES_SEED in Code.gs are
   empty, and the fallback bus pairing in both Code.gs and the Worker is
   blank. Your tabs already hold all of it, so nothing you see changes.
   What does change: if the Route in odd months column is ever cleared, a
   new Sunday gets no bus in its cell rather than a guess, and a cleared
   Lat or Lng on Bus Stops is not refilled (type it back in).

2. SETTING UP FOR A NEW CHURCH.
   A checklist in README.md of everything another church changes: its
   copies, its settings, the addresses and details still in config.js and
   Code.gs, and the four tabs it fills in.

3. THE COORDINATOR'S APP, UPRIGHT AND CLEAR OF THE CLOCK.
   Turned on its side it says "Turn your phone upright", as the driver app
   always has (iPhones ignore the "portrait only" an app asks for, so this
   is the only way).
   On a Home Screen iPhone, going to the driver app and back made Safari
   report no room for the status bar, and the bar slid under the clock and
   signal. The app now remembers the room it measured when it opened and
   puts it back when Safari says none. Nothing to reinstall.
