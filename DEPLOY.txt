WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. THE COORDINATOR'S APP KEEPS THE LAST DRIVERS TAB ON THE PHONE.
   Since config.js stopped carrying names (v1.80.0), the sign-in screen

   the cursor arrived a second late. It now keeps the last list the live
   server gave it, as the driver app keeps its register, draws it at once
   and replaces it with every answer. A name added, taken off or given a
   new role on the Drivers tab still reaches it on the next open with a
   signal.
