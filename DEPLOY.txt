WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. BUS LINK FOR THIS SUNDAY. The passenger page's address, with a button
   that opens WhatsApp ready to send it to a person or the group, and one
   to copy it. Built in the app itself; it needs neither server.

2. IS THE LIVE SERVER WORKING? and WHO IS TAPPING. Built by the sheet, the
   same code as the menu items, handed back through the live server after
   it has checked the PIN. The menu items are unchanged.

3. SEND DUTY REMINDERS NOW. The same as the menu item: sends only the
   reminders due today, and never the same one twice. The app asks
   "Send the duty reminders due today?" first, and has no Ask again on the
   answer, so it cannot be sent by a stray tap.

4. A TEST THAT FAILED AFTER NINE AT NIGHT. One coordinator-alert test ran
   on the real clock and, in the quiet hours, rightly saw a non-urgent
   alert held for the morning. It now runs at two in the afternoon. The
   app was never wrong.

5. TIME TO SET OFF, AND NO WORD YET.
   On the departure time (North 09:52 and South 10:21 on today's timetable,
   whatever the Bus Stops tab says), the rostered driver's phone gets
   "Time to set off. Your North run is due to leave church at 09:52. Tap
   Start as you pull away." Before, the first word was ten minutes late.
   The ten and twenty-five minute reminders are unchanged.
   Five minutes past, if nobody has tapped Start, everybody booked on that
   route gets "No word yet that your bus has left church. It was due to
   leave at 09:52. Yours is timetabled 10:52 at Stanley Pk. We will message
   you as soon as it sets off." Once, and not on a route called off, during
   a rehearsal, or more than an hour past.
   WHY THOSE WORDS. Start is the only thing that tells the live server a bus
   has gone. A driver who pulls away without tapping it is on the road while
   the record says he is at church, so "the bus has not left" or "the driver
   is running behind" could both be untrue, and the second points at a
   person. "No word yet" is true either way. The moment Start is tapped,
   they get "The bus has left church" as before.
   Nothing to set up. It needs the Worker's every-minute Cron Trigger, which
   you already have.
