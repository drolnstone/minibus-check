MINIBUS
=======
  pages v1.99.0 · server w2.44.0 · sheet v1.104.0

Driver app, Stops and bookings (Asim): a driver who is not driving that
route taps a stop and follows the bus to it, on North or South, the way a
passenger without a booking can on the passenger page.

  - The panel above the list says what the passenger page says about that
    stop: minutes away, Be at your stop, gone past, picked up.
  - Tap the stop again, or Stop following, to stop. Kept for the Sunday.
  - Not on the route this phone is driving, and not during a rehearsal.

This sits on top of w2.44.0 (stop list keeps timetable order, PR #50).
