Pages and server.

All three apps take a new version without being closed and reopened.
While open they look for one every minute and each time they come back
to the screen. When one is ready the app reloads once, at a quiet
moment: not while something is being typed, a pop-up is open, or taps
are waiting to send. The Driver App also waits while on the check or
trip-marking screens; the Sunday Bus page waits while live times show.

A stop moved to the other route for the day: a Picked up or Nobody here
for it from the run it left is not recorded, so that run does not count
it as served and its passengers get no "Nobody there". Taps from the run
it joined are recorded as usual. 22 Nov: the North Driver App, opened
before the fixes, still listed the Nursery; North marked it Nobody here
at 10:43 after South picked it up at 10:32.

Merging is the whole deploy.
