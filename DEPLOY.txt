  3.  Minibus menu     ->  Rota and setup

Steps 1 to 3 need a computer. Step 4 can be done from a phone.

step 3 lands on a Worker that knows what to do with the sheet version.


WHAT CHANGED
------------

  *  THE OFFER OF ALERTS IS NOW ASKED, NOT POSTED.

     It was a grey line under the buttons in the driver app and a grey line
     under the bus panel on the passenger page. It is now a question over a
     dimmed screen, with Not now and Turn on.

     Once each time the app is opened, until they turn alerts on or the
     phone refuses. Not now costs them nothing until the next launch.

     IT NEVER COVERS THE VEHICLE CHECK. In the driver app it is drawn on the
     first screen after he has picked his name, and nowhere else. It cannot
     appear while a check is open, cannot appear on top of another sheet,
     and closes on either button, on the backdrop and on Escape. Nothing in
     the check flow can reach it.

     On the passenger page it is only ever shown to somebody who has
     actually booked a seat for the coming Sunday. A page open on a
     stranger's phone is asked for nothing.

     On an iPhone in a tab it asks the other question instead, because that
     phone cannot be given an alert at all until the app is on the Home
     Screen. The button then opens the steps.

     NOT RED. Red in these apps means a defect or a bus off the road. The
     sheet stops the eye by standing in front of the screen, which costs no
     colour. If you still want red, say so and it is one line.

     TO TURN IT OFF: alertPopup in config.js for the driver app, and
     ALERT_POPUP near the top of sunday/index.html for the passenger page.
     Set either to false and that app goes back to the line. The passenger
     page has its own copy because it loads no config.js: it is opened from
     a WhatsApp link by members and has no business downloading the driver
     register to do it. Keep the two in step.

  *  ALL THREE VERSION NUMBERS ARE ON ONE LINE, IN BOTH APPS.

     The foot of the driver app's first screen, and the foot of the
     passenger page, now read:

         app v1.65.0 · server w2.1.0 · sheet v1.60.0

     On a narrow phone it wraps between the three, never through the middle
     of a number.

     Before this, "script" meant the Worker in the apps and the Apps Script
     in the spreadsheet. Two different numbers wearing one word is what made
     you open the spreadsheet to check the third.

     The Worker cannot see the spreadsheet, so it is told: every sync now
     carries the Apps Script's version, the Worker parks it and hands it
     back on every reply. A slot nobody has named stays blank rather than
     guessing.

     Minibus -> Have a look -> Is everything working? now reports the live
     server's version too, so the sheet and the phones agree.

  *  THAT SAME REPORT NOW NAMES WHO HAS NOT GOT ALERTS ON.

         Still to do:
           *  No alerts yet for: Bro Moses, Bro Adesina.
              Open the app on that phone and tap Turn on.
         Fine:
           *  Alerts on: 7 of 9 drivers, 14 passenger phones.

     There was no way to find this out before except to take each phone and
     press the bell. You are about to ask nine drivers to turn alerts on. This
     is how you see whether they did.

     Named in the Drivers tab's Order column. Retired drivers are not chased.

     A driver with no alerts is not a fault. He may have said no. The group
     message still reaches him. So the report now has a third bucket, Still
     to do, and only Needs attention counts in the dialog title. Without it
     this report would open with "Minibus: 1 thing to look at" every week
     until the last man tapped Turn on.

  *  A DRIVER WHOSE NAME IS SPELLED DIFFERENTLY ON THE ROTA IS NOW WOKEN.

     The sweep that sends a driver his alerts looked up his handset by an
     exact match on the name in the ROTA cell. A cover typed in by hand that
     did not match the Drivers tab character for character meant that driver
     was never woken, and nothing anywhere said so. It is now matched the way
     a person would read it, lower cased and trimmed, and the report uses the
     very same rule so the two cannot drift apart again.

     This can only ever find MORE handsets than before, never fewer.


AFTER DEPLOYING
---------------

  *  The foot of the app's first screen should read
     app v1.65.0 · server w2.1.0 · sheet v1.60.0.

     Same three at the foot of the passenger page.

     A blank sheet slot means step 3 has not run.

  *  Minibus -> Have a look -> Is everything working?

     Under Fine: "Script is v1.60.0." and "Live server is w2.1.0."

     Under Still to do, before anybody has turned alerts on, it should name
     every driver on the register. That is correct. The title should say
     "still to do", NOT "things to look at": if it says things to look at,
     something else in the report is genuinely wrong.

  *  OPEN THE DRIVER APP AND PICK YOUR NAME on a phone that does not have
     reminders on. The question should come up on the hub. Tap Not now, go
     into Vehicle check, come back: it must not come again this launch.

  *  Turn reminders on and open the app again. No question.

  *  Open the passenger page, book a seat, and the question should follow a
     moment later. Not before you book.

  *  Check the sheet in a dark theme and in green or navy.


IF THE POP-UP IS WRONG FOR SUNDAY
---------------------------------

Set alertPopup false in config.js, set ALERT_POPUP false in
sunday/index.html, bump CACHE in both sw.js files and redeploy the pages.
Both apps go back to the line and nothing else changes.
