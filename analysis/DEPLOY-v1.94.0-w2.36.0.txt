   MOT run. Test time, Authorise. History has "MOT run authorised". In the

   until the new MOT is recorded.

   active Coordinator and Minister in Charge on the Drivers tab.
   COORDINATOR_EMAIL may hold several addresses, separated by commas.


WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. The stop. worker.js papersStop and papersToday; index.html papersOf;
   coord/index.html needs(). A date is good to the end of the day it names.
   Insurance: no way round it. MOT: one MOT run. Service and permit: warning
   only, as before. A run started on a stopped bus from a phone with old
   dates is still taken (refusing it would hold every tap behind it), and
   every coordinator's phone is told at once.

2. The MOT run. coord/index.html motRun; worker.js actMotrun (a coordinator
   action, kind motrun) and handleMotrun (action motrun, from the driver
   app); index.html motRunHtml. Allowed only with a standing MOT booking on
   the Vehicle Log for that bus for today. Garage from the booking. The test
   time is typed when authorising (the Vehicle Log has no time column); a
   time in the booking's notes is filled in. One per bus per day. Under way,
   it takes the place of the route on the run screen. No passengers: it is
   not a route run, so it carries no bookings.

3. History. Code.gs coordMotrun writes "MOT run authorised" with the
   coordinator, garage, time and booking. A defect authorisation from the
   app now writes "Defect authorised".

4. Renewals on the first screen. coord/index.html renewalsHtml.

5. Alerts. Code.gs safetyAlert, safetyTo, renewalAlerts, missedTapAlert.
   Each once, to every coordinator's phone and to safetyTo().
   - Renewals at 60, 30 and 7 days and on the day, then weekly once
     overdue, at 08:00. Expired MOT or insurance says BUS STOPPED.
   - A booking on the Vehicle Log for tomorrow, at 08:00.
   - A date on the Buses tab the sheet cannot read.
   - A due date typed on the Buses tab, not through the app.
   - A Vehicle Log entry corrected or withdrawn.
   - A defect reopened.
   - An MOT run authorised.
   - A booked stop not marked, when a run ends.
   - The walkaround stop email goes to safetyTo() too, at any hour, as
     before.
   - The weekly summary lists renewals due in 60 days or overdue.
   An alert raised by an edit on the sheet goes with the next five-minute
   sync.

6. Tests. tests/suites/46-papers.mjs.
