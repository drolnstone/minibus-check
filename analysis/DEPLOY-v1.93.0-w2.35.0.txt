WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. Buses. coord/index.html editBus; worker.js actBus, laid over the buses
   table by getBuses until the sheet files it; Code.gs coordBus. A bus that
   takes another active bus's route in odd months gives that bus its old
   route, or standby. Notes longer than 500 characters stay on the tab.

2. Rota notes. coord/index.html editNote; worker.js actRota noteEdit,
   rotaApplySet; Code.gs coordRota. One line of the Notes cell changed or
   taken off. A line longer than 300 characters is never cut. A Notes cell
   left with one line such as 11/10 or 10:30 stays as typed.

3. Bus stops. coord/index.html paintStops and editStop; worker.js actStop,
   the stops table changed at once and laid back over each push
   (reapplyStops); Code.gs coordStopsList and coordStop. A new stop takes the
   next number on its route and its place by time. A time out of order, a
   second Depart or Arrival, or switching off a stop with seats booked for
   a run still to come is refused. A new postcode clears Lat and Lng;
   spacing and capitals are not a new postcode. Switched-off rows do not
   decide where a new stop goes.

4. tests/browser/coordinator.mjs C38 expects "was", as the Run record says
   from v1.90.2.
