WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. EVERY SCREEN SAYS ITS NAME ONCE. The heading that repeated the bar is gone
   from Rota, Rota requests, Bookings, Defects, Buses, Run record, Have a
   look, What has been done and Rehearsal, and from the driver app's Driving
   rota, Stops and bookings and Waiting to send. A Sunday keeps its date, a
   bus its plate and a report its name. (coord/index.html, index.html;
   browser checks C37 and T21; sabotages S32 and S40.)

2. THE ROW OF SUNDAYS STAYS IN VIEW on Bookings and the Run record, pinned
   under the bar while the list scrolls. (coord/index.html .chips.stick,
   barHeight; C34; S30.)

3. ONE BACK. Switching Sunday on Bookings or the Run record replaces the page
   instead of adding one, so five Sundays no longer take five Backs.
   (coord/index.html go; C35; S29.)

4. THE ROTA FROM THE SUNDAY CARD. A Sunday opened from the first screen is
   opened from inside the Rota: Back is the list of Sundays. The Rota button
   is gone; the card says ROTA at its corner. (go, sunCard, paintHome; C36;
   S31.)

5. WHAT HAS BEEN DONE, not What I have done: the screen always showed every
   coordinator's changes, each with who made it. (C36, C37.)

6. ONE CARD PER FAULT. Repeated reports of one item on one bus are one card:
   the heaviest weight any report gave it, the newest words, how many reports
   since when, how long open, the DVSA tag, and every trail. Update and Close
   are for every report, ticked; one Close with one "what was done" closes
   them all, each with its own History line. The first screen, the Buses
   screens and Record something count and tick faults, not reports. The
   driver app stops showing "Still open on this bus" for a fault closed in
   one go. (coord/index.html defectGroups, paintDefects, updateDefect,
   DVSA_ITEMS held to the driver app's list by a test; worker.js actDefect
   keys, defectKeysOf; Code.gs coordDefect; C39; S33 and S34.)

7. THE RUN RECORD KNOWS WHAT WAS BOOKED. Each stop by its number, with the
   seats booked there that Sunday by number. An untapped stop with seats is
   amber, "N seats booked, not marked"; one with nobody booked is grey, since
   the driver app asks for no tap there. A number that was another place
   that morning says "then" and the old place. (worker.js coordRuns;
   coord/index.html evRow; C38; S35 and S36.)

8. A STOP IS ITS NUMBER, AND THE GUARDS FOR CHANGING THE PLACE BEHIND ONE.
   A booking and a tap keep the name their number had when they were made;
   it is compared, never matched on. When it no longer matches:
     - History has every change to a numbered row of the Bus Stops tab (the
       place, the time, Active, a number added or taken off), from an edit,
       a paste or a script (Code.gs stopsAudit, onEditBusStops);
     - the sheet says at once which seats for a coming Sunday were booked at
       the old place, and History's line names them;
     - Is everything working? lists them under To do (stopsHealth);
     - the coordinator's Bookings says "Booked when N05 was …" (C40; S37).
   The passenger is told nothing: the stops and their numbers are the
   coordinator's business.
   Stops are shown with their number on the Run record, Bookings and
   booking for somebody.

9. NO TEACHING ON THE SCREENS. The coordinator's app states facts, asks
   questions and names buttons; the how and the why are for the manual and
   the videos. Gone: "Only what it was is needed…", "It goes on the log as
   booked…", how each next due date was worked out (it stays on the Vehicle
   Log tab), the offer to line a service up with the MOT, the rehearsal's
   explanations, the duty reminders' and the bus link's, "Past Sundays are…",
   the long half of two error messages, and the sample text in the note
   fields ("Topped up after the service", "The date was typed wrong"), which
   was only ever a sample and never sent.
