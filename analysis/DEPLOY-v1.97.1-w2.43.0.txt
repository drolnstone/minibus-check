A coordinator with both apps is alerted once, not twice (Asim):

  - Coordinator alerts go to the coordinator app when the person has it,
    and to the driver app only when they do not.
  - A driver's own reminders (set off, End trip, Sunday morning) go to the
    driver app only.
  - Each app tells the server which one it is when it opens, so the
    doubles stop once both apps have been opened after the merge.
