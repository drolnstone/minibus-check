WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. BOOKINGS KEEP THE TIME. bookings.sched (added by the Worker on first
   use), filled when a seat is booked or moved to another stop. Changing
   only the seats keeps it. Bus Bookings, Scheduled column, as text.

2. EARLY OR LATE. A tap at a booked stop is measured from the first live
   booking's time there. A cancelled seat's time no longer counts. The same
   on the Worker, on an Added time in the coordinator's app, and on the
   sheet's own tap path.

3. RUN RECORD. The left column shows the time the figure was measured
   from: the tap's own, else the booked time, else today's timetable.

4. A tap revived after Undo takes the new figure too.

Bookings made before this have no time on them, so their stops go by the
timetable until they are changed.
