MINIBUS
=======
  pages v1.96.0 · server w2.39.0 · sheet v1.103.0

Drivers can change their PIN, copied from the Ushers app. Every driver
starts on the default PIN, the last four digits of their phone number on
the Drivers tab. The first time they sign in after this, the app asks
"Do you wish to keep your default PIN?". They can change it any time with
Change PIN on the hub. Reset PIN in the coordinator app puts a driver back
on the default. The PIN column on the Drivers tab is no longer used:
delete it at the end.

It changes how drivers sign in, so you may prefer to deploy it after
Sunday's run.

BEFORE YOU START
----------------

On the Drivers tab, check that every active driver has a phone number,
and that their PIN column matches the last four digits of their phone.
From step 2 the phone number is what counts. A driver with no phone
number and no PIN of their own is not asked for a PIN at all.

AFTER IT
--------

A. The foot of all three apps reads  app v1.96.0 · server w2.39.0 · sheet
   v1.103.0 .

B. Minibus › Is everything working?: "Every active driver has a phone
   number, so a default PIN" is under Fine. Anyone without one is named
   under Needs attention.

C. Driver app: sign in with the last four digits of your phone. On the
   way to the hub it asks "Do you wish to keep your default PIN?".
   Tap Yes, keep it. You are on the hub, and are not asked again.

D. On the hub, tap Change PIN (small, under the buttons). Key your current
   PIN, a new one twice, Change PIN. "PIN changed." Close the app, open it,
   sign in with the new PIN: it works, and the old one does not.

E. Coordinator app › Drivers › your name › Reset PIN, OK. You are back on
   the phone digits, and the driver app asks the keep question again.

F. History tab: a line each for C, D and E (Default PIN kept, PIN changed,
   PIN reset). No PIN is written anywhere.


WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. server/worker.js: driver_pins (made on first use): a driver's own PIN,
   a random salt each, PBKDF2-SHA-256 with PIN_SALT as the pepper, as the
   Ushers app does it. Every PIN check (sign-in, authorising a bus, ending
   another run, the coordinator's app, the email link) takes a driver's own
   PIN before his default. New actions pinchange and pinkeep; the
   coordinator's change kind "pin" resets. The sign-in answer carries
   askPinChange. The rota and the Drivers screen count an own PIN.

2. Code.gs: the default PIN is phoneDefaultPin(Phone); the PIN column is
   not read and not asked for. The sheet no longer answers a PIN (the app
   uses the live server, then its own copy). Kind "pin" writes the History
   line. The health check names drivers with no phone number.

3. index.html: the keep question and the Change PIN screen; the quiet
   re-check asks the live server. coord/index.html: Reset PIN on a driver.

4. Tests: tests/suites/49-driver-pin.mjs.
