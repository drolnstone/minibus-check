Coordinator alerts, after the Ushers app's notifications (its PRs #18 and
#20). For coordinators only:

  - The coordinator app asks ONCE, on its first screen, "Turn on alerts on
    this phone?" (an iPhone in Safari is asked to add the app to its Home
    Screen first). Not now is never asked again on that phone; the bell
    does it any time.
  - The bell shows a red count of alerts not yet seen, and the same count
    is on the first screen's new Alerts line and, where the phone allows,
    on the app's home-screen icon.
  - The filled bell now opens Alerts: the last two days of coordinator
    alerts, the new ones marked. Send a test alert is there now, not on the
    bell itself.
  - While the app is open, a new alert shows in a strip at the foot of the
    screen (red for a stopped bus). Tap it to open Alerts, or close it. The
    page makes room for it, so it never covers a button.
  - A coordinator with alerts on NO phone is emailed the alert's short
    words at once, if they have an Email on the Drivers tab. Whoever
    COORDINATOR_EMAIL names already gets the full email and is not sent it
    twice. Never for the menu's test.
