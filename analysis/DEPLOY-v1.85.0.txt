WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. RENEWAL DATES ARE CELLS.
   An MOT or insurance renewal used to mean editing config.js and
   publishing the pages. Now it is typing a date on the Buses tab and Send
   everything (or waiting for the next automatic send). Leave a cell blank
   for anything a bus does not have. The driver app warns 30 days ahead, red
   once passed, exactly as before.

2. THE MONTHLY PAIRING IS A CELL.
   "Route in odd months" says which bus takes North and which South in
   January, March and so on; even months are the other way round. Swap the
   two words to swap the rotation. A bus typed on a Sunday's Rota row still
   wins for that Sunday. A standby bus is left blank. If the column does not
   have exactly one active North and one active South, the old pairing is
   used rather than a guess.

3. A new bus is now a row: registration, seats, Active YES, its dates and,
   if it takes a route, North or South. Its checklist details (name, what
   it skips) are still in config.js.
