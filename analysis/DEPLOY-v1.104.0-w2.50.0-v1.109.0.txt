MINIBUS

  pages v1.104.0 · server w2.50.0 · sheet v1.109.0

Photos on bus reports (Asim):
- Defect: at least one photo is needed before the stage moves on.
- Advisory: Add photo is there, not needed.
- Fine: no photo.
- Items a photo cannot show never ask: Keys and remote, First aid kit,
  Documents on board, Brakes and pedals, Steering, Clutch and gears,
  "Horn, heater, demist", Driver's seat and belt, Speed limiter.
- Up to 3 an item. Add photo opens the phone's camera or its photos.
  Each is shrunk on the phone to about 250 KB before it is sent.
- The check goes first; the photos follow. With no signal they wait on
  the phone, and the red bar says how many.
- Coordinators see them on the defect's card in the coordinator app.
  Tap one for the full photo, tap again to close.
- The Defects tab gets a Photos column with the count. No photo goes on
  the sheet or in Drive.
- Kept while the defect is open; otherwise 26 weeks, as the tab.
