WHAT IS NEW IN v1.74.0
----------------------

  1.  A BUS CAN BE AUTHORISED FROM THE EMAIL.

      The "BUS STOPPED" message now carries a button. It opens a page, the
      page shows the check, and it asks for your PIN. The same three tries and
      the same five minute lockout as the app.

      Authorising this way is the SAME ACT as authorising in the app, writing
      the same record. The defect stays open on the Defects tab with your name
      against the decision to run.

  2.  A ROTA REQUEST CAN BE APPROVED OR TURNED DOWN FROM THE EMAIL.

      Same page, same PIN, two buttons.

      It does NOT write the Rota directly. It records your answer, the next
      five minute sync carries it to the Rota Requests tab, and the tab's own
      handler writes the Rota — the very code path you go through by typing in
      the Status cell. So a swap still moves both Sundays, a protected Sunday
      is still refused, and the route still comes from the requester. There is
      no second version of "what approving means" to drift out of step.

      The row gets a note saying it was decided from the email link, by whom,
      and when.

      PICKING A REPLACEMENT IS STILL BY HAND, deliberately. Choosing who
      covers a Sunday is a judgement about people made looking at the rota,
      and a dropdown in an email is not where it belongs.

  3.  IT IS NEVER A ONE-CLICK LINK, AND THAT IS THE POINT.

      A link that acts when it is opened is acted on by whatever opens it.
      Your mail provider scans links for malware. Phones fetch them to build
      previews. Corporate gateways rewrite them and follow them to see where
      they go. Any of those would have authorised a bus with a fault on it
      while the email was still unread.

      So opening the link changes nothing at all. It shows you the decision.
      Only the PIN acts. A scanner may open it fifty times and the bus stays
      off the road — there is a test that does exactly that.

  4.  ONE USE, AND ABOUT AN HOUR.

      A mailbox is kept for years. A link that still works in March is a way
      into the record that nobody is watching. After it is used, or after
      LINK_RULES.ttlMinutes, the page says so and offers nothing.

      A wrong PIN does NOT use the link up, so a mistyped one is just a
      mistyped one.

  5.  THE PAGE ITSELF.

      New file, do/index.html. No service worker, no manifest, no config.js,
      nothing loaded from anywhere but the live server. It is opened once,
      from a message, and closed.

      Nothing caches it, on purpose: a decision served from a cache is a
      decision made against a stale picture of the morning. If there is no
      signal it does not load, and a page that does not load is a better
      answer here than one that loads and is wrong.

  WHOSE PIN IT ASKS FOR is whoever COORDINATOR_EMAIL matches in the Email
  column of the Drivers tab. Not a new setting: change who gets the emails and
  the link follows, with nothing to keep in step. If no Email is filled in it
  falls back to the first active coordinator.

  TO TURN IT ALL OFF: LINK_RULES.on = false in Code.gs. Every email goes back
  to saying "open the spreadsheet" and nothing else changes.

  IF THE LIVE SERVER IS DOWN when an email is built, the email still goes,
  without a link, saying to use the spreadsheet. A convenience must never be
  able to stop you finding out that a bus is off the road.
