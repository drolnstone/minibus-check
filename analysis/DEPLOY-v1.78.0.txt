WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. A PASSENGER WITH A SEAT IS REMINDED WHAT HE BOOKED.
   The reminder run skipped anyone with a booking, so his only reminder was
   the Sunday morning message. Now, on Saturday between 18:00 and 19:00, every
   phone with alerts on and a seat for Sunday is told, once:

       You are booked for Sunday
       Grace Road bus stop, Walton Vale, 10:23. 2 seats. Tap to change or
       cancel.

   People with no seat still get the three nudges as before.

   It is a setting: booked: true on the Saturday window in BOOKING_RULES
   (Code.gs) and booking (config.js), which must match. Put it on another
   window as well and that window reminds too. Take it off and it stops.

2. THE HUB, FOR A COORDINATOR.
   Coordinator has a line of its own, as wide as Vehicle check. Driving rota
   and Stops and bookings stay together, and Vehicle check stays the
   full-width button at the bottom. A driver's hub is unchanged.

3. NO SECOND PIN FROM THE HUB.
   A coordinator whose PIN the driver app has checked goes straight to the
   coordinator's first screen when he taps Coordinator. The PIN goes once, in
   that tab's own session storage, and the coordinator's page takes it and
   wipes it as it opens; a copy more than a minute old is not used. With no
   checked PIN, only the name goes and the page asks. Opened from its own
   icon or a link, it always asks. It still locks after 15 minutes untouched,
   and the live server still checks the PIN on every call.

4. THE PIN BOX IS FOCUSED ONCE A NAME IS CHOSEN, as on the driver app.

5. THE COORDINATOR'S LANDING PAGE.
   The sign-in screen carries the three numbers, read from the live server
   before anyone signs in, and the names that may sign in now come from the
   Drivers tab the live server holds, with config.js only until it answers.
   Tapping the numbers gives the driver app's kind of report: the versions,
   whether the live server answered, who can open the page, whether the
   chosen name has a PIN, how this visit was opened, the lock and refresh
   settings, and after a sign-in the changes on their way to the sheet and
   when the clock last ticked. The theme chips are under the numbers and are
   the driver app's own setting, so a theme set in either app is set in both.
   The first screen after signing in no longer carries the numbers.
