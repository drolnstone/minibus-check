WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. coord/sw.js (new): the coordinator's app's own offline copy, in its own
   folder, as the passenger page has. It keeps the page, config.js and the
   logo, always tries the network first, and never touches the live server's
   answers. The coordinator's alerts stay with the driver app's worker.

2. coord/index.html: registers it; says "No connection just now" on the
   first screen when there is none; tries again on its own once the signal
   is back, including a PIN carried over from the driver app. A new copy
   takes over at once on the sign-in screen, and is otherwise picked up the
   next time the app is opened, so nobody is signed out mid-change.

3. sw.js, sunday/sw.js, index.html, sunday/index.html: the version only.
   sw.js's note about the coordinator's folder now points at coord/sw.js.

4. Tests. tests/suites/01-stamps.mjs checks the new worker's version and
   that it never caches the live server. tests/browser/coordinator.mjs C49
   opens the app offline and back online.
