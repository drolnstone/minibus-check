WHAT CHANGED
------------

1. The reports. Code.gs pdfReport gathers each one as tables from the tabs:
   - Outstanding (as at today): buses stopped (expired MOT or insurance, a
     critical defect), renewals overdue or due in 60 days, open defects and
     advisories, bookings on the Vehicle Log, rota gaps in the next 8
     weeks, rota requests waiting.
   - Fleet and safety record (From, To): each bus and its dates, walkaround
     checks, defects reported or closed, the Vehicle Log, and History rows
     about a bus.
   - Sunday report (one Sunday): the rota, each run (left church, ended),
     each stop marked against its time, seats booked by stop, walkaround
     checks, defects reported that day.
   - Period summary (From, To): counts, each route (runs, seats, stops,
     average early or late), each driver (Sundays driven, covers, checks).
   Quick dates: This month, Last month, Last 3 months, This year.

2. The PDF. coord/pdf.js lays it out with coord/jspdf.umd.min.js (jsPDF
   4.2.1, MIT, loaded only when a PDF is made). A4, real text, the logo,
   title, period, who made it and when. Every page: "Contains names" and
   "Page x of y". File name: Minibus - <report> - <dates>.pdf.

3. Drive. The app sends the PDF through the live server (coord ops pdf and
   pdfsave, after the PIN) to Code.gs pdfSave, which keeps it in the
   "Minibus reports" folder beside the spreadsheet (Script Properties
   REPORTS_FOLDER holds its id; set it to another folder's id to use that
   one). The folder is shared with nobody: the files are yours, and the
   coordinator who made one also has it on his phone.

4. No typing boxes are added: the period is chosen with date pickers.

5. Tests. tests/suites/47-pdf-reports.mjs.
