When somebody is put on emergency calls in the coordinator app, everybody
with a coordinator title is told at once, the person picked most of all:

  - A phone alert, "Emergency calls: Bro Calvin today", saying who set it,
    until when, and the number every Call button now shows.
  - The same line in the coordinator app's Alerts, with the red count on
    the bell, for anybody whose phone has alerts off.
  - Putting it back tells them too: "Emergency calls: back to Bro Asim".
  - Whoever made the change is not told their own change.
  - It goes at once, even in the quiet hours before 08:00.

No email: the live server does not send email, and adding one would need
Code.gs. Somebody with alerts off on every phone sees it in Alerts.

This follows v1.96.8 · w2.41.0 · v1.104.0 (choose who takes emergency
calls).
