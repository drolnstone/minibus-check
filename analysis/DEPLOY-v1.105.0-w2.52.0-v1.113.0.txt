Record something takes several things at once (Asim, 11 Oct):

  - On a bus page, Record something: tap as many of MOT, Service,
    Insurance, Parking permit, Repair, Tyres and Other as were done (or
    booked) on the visit. A second tap takes one off.
  - Record it puts one entry on the Vehicle Log for each, with the same
    Done or Booked ahead, day, mileage, garage and notes.
  - Each one chosen has its own cost box (MOT cost, Service cost ...), and
    each dated item its own date box (MOT: expiry date on the certificate
    ...). The blue box shows each one's next due date.
  - A defect ticked as put right goes on the Repair entry if Repair is
    chosen, otherwise on the first one.

Pages only. The Worker and Code.gs do not change.
