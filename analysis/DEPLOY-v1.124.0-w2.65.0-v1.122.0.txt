Asim, 16 Oct: the mileage when the bus is put away after the afternoon run,
so the next walkaround shows any use during the week. An exact match, a
column of its own, and only on the phone of the driver who drove the bus
that morning. He chose "Optional reading" on the card.

1. Driver app, on a Sunday: once that morning's run has ended, the first
   screen has a "Bus parked" box for that bus, on that driver's phone only.
   With no run in the app that day, it shows from noon to the driver who did
   the walkaround. It goes at midnight. Keying it needs the driver's PIN, the
   same as a run. It can be changed until midnight.
2. With no signal, the reading stays on the phone ("Saved on this phone.
   Sent when there is signal.") and goes by itself once the app is open with
   signal, up to a week later, as long as that bus has had no newer
   walkaround. It is filed with the time it was keyed. A closed iPhone app
   cannot send, so it goes the next time the app is opened with signal. If
   it reaches the sheet after the next walkaround, that walkaround's Since
   parked is worked out again from it.
3. Notifications, to that morning's driver only, with the coordinators'
   usual "Sent:" copy:
   - "Trip ended" when the server ends the run. While the bus has no parked
     reading it adds: "Record the bus parked mileage when you return from
     the afternoon run."
   - At 2pm, "Parked mileage", only if nothing has been keyed yet, naming
     each bus still owed. Not after 3pm, and not in the half hour after
     Trip ended.
   - "Parked mileage received" once the reading is in.
4. Checks tab: two new columns beside Mileage. "Parked mileage" is filled on
   the Sunday walkaround's row when the reading comes in, with a note of who
   keyed it and when. "Since parked" is filled on the next walkaround's row:
   the miles since the bus was parked. 0 means it has not moved. Anything
   else, even 1 mile, goes to the coordinators' phones as an alert.
5. Nothing keyed on the Sunday: "Since parked" says "Not reported" (blank
   after a weekday walkaround, when none could be keyed). Its note gives the
   miles since the last walkaround and a usual Sunday's miles for that bus.
   Only a real big gap, more than one and a half usual Sundays, goes to the
   coordinators' phones.
6. Coordinator app, Buses: each bus shows its last walkaround and its parked
   reading, or "Parked mileage not reported since the last walkaround".

The first Sunday after this goes live only collects parked readings. The
walkaround the Sunday after is the first one compared with them.
